Pagewake

Know when a web page changes.

Pagewake is a Chrome extension that checks the pages you choose and tells you when they change: a price, a restock, a job list, a permit status. It shows exactly what changed, word by word. No account, no cloud: your watch list never leaves your browser.

How it works

  1. Open a page and click the Pagewake icon.
  2. Watch the whole page, or point at one element: a price, a table, a block of text.
  3. When it changes, you get a desktop notification and a clear before/after view.

Questions

Where is my data?
In your browser only. Pagewake has no server and no account. Read the privacy policy.
Which pages can it watch?
Public pages. Pages behind a login, a captcha or strict anti-bot protection can't be watched, and Pagewake says so instead of failing silently. Pages built with JavaScript work with the "Live render" option.
Does it check when Chrome is closed?
No. Checks run while Chrome is open, and missed checks run shortly after you reopen it.
I paid on another computer. How do I get Pro here?
Open the dashboard, then Settings, then "Already paid? Restore", and log in with the email you used to pay.
